Privacy Policy

This page explains what information EventOps AI handles and how it is used when you use the platform.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Information the platform handles

Depending on the features you use, EventOps AI may store or process:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and password hash.
  • Event registration details, including the event, ticket type, registration status, and check-in status.
  • Feedback you submit, including a rating and optional comment.
  • Messages sent through Contact, including your name, email address, topic, and message.
  • Text you enter in the AI assistant.

How information is used

Information is used to create and maintain accounts, authenticate users, manage event registrations, display your registrations and feedback, respond to contact messages, and provide the platform’s event-management features.

AI assistant

If you use the AI assistant, the text you submit may be sent to Google’s Gemini service so it can interpret your request and generate a response. Avoid including passwords or other sensitive information in assistant messages. The assistant may also use information available in EventOps AI to answer requests about events or your registrations.

Cookies and external services

The platform uses a session cookie to support sign-in and form security. The site also loads interface libraries from jsDelivr; those requests may be subject to that service’s own privacy practices.

Storage and security

Platform records are stored in the database configured for this deployment. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable text. Data retention and backup practices depend on the operator’s deployment and database configuration.

Your questions or requests

For questions about information associated with your account, or to request a correction or deletion, contact the platform team through the Contact page.
